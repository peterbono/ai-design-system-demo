A team's Bridge says who gets in: one screen per GitHub access state (sign in first, no access with "Ask your lead for access", Bridge not set up with the install link for admins only, read only outside the team), a team chip at the top of the Studio side nav with your access, and "Invite your team" (the team link, "Who can do what", Manage access on GitHub for admins) as an onboarding step and in Settings. Onboarding is remembered per repository, and step 2 shows the hosted states.
