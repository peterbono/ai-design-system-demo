Studio: "Create a new team" in the team chip menu now opens a dialog over the page instead of leaving for the launcher on Studio home, so you keep your place. Paste owner/repo or a GitHub address; after a short pause a calm line says what you will find there (Ready, Read only, Bridge is not installed yet with "Install Bridge" for admins, Not found or private, or Sign in to check). The dialog and the Studio home launcher share one parser, the same error words and the same team address (lib/team-open.ts).
