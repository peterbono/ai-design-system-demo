Studio Home: when nothing waits on you, the Harbor zone is no longer an empty box. A Field Guide harbor scene (the bridge with its flag, a moored sailboat with someone fishing, a cloud passing the sun, two gulls) draws itself in once, then breathes in slow, staggered loops built on the slowest motion token; with reduced motion it stays a still drawing. The line under the greeting reads "Nothing needs you. The harbor is calm." The scene is a reusable QuietScene component with a `scene` prop, for other calm states later.
