Designers write only to their own explorations in a product's design copy, also after the copy is made again: a pull request belongs to whoever owns its branch (never to an owner kept by its number, which a recreated copy reuses), and an exploration re-opened by a lead stays its designer's when its exploration block names them. A new pull request's block always names who opened it.
