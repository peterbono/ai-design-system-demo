The toolbar reads a change of a private product: Bridge Studio hands a member's tab a short-lived read pass in the link's fragment, the toolbar takes it out of the address, keeps it for the tab and sends it with its reads through Bridge. When the change still cannot be read, Live product keeps working for a code change, the toolbar names the change by its branch (else "This exploration") and points back to Studio instead of asking for a reload.
