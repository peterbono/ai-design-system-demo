One Bridge account per Gmail mailbox: emails are keyed by their canonical spelling (lower case; for gmail.com and googlemail.com without dots or a "+tag"), so either spelling signs in to the same account. Accounts made under two spellings before are merged safely on the next sign-in or account read: the older identity and its GitHub link stay, memberships, invites, join requests, notifications and connections in Claude move, and two different GitHub accounts are never merged.
