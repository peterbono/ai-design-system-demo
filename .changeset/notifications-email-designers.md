Designers who joined by email now hear about their own work: an email right away when a developer asks them a question, when the developers' version is ready for their look, or when a lead asks for changes on their library suggestion, and a daily summary at 9:00 in their time zone for what moved (declined, shipped, suggestion closed). Account settings has a new Email row to choose right away, daily or never, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Sent through Brevo (`BREVO_API_KEY`, `NOTIFY_FROM`); without the key nothing is sent.
