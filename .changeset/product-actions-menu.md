Product actions in Studio. In Start an exploration, the Where list ends with "Add a product" after a separator, a plus in the link color, for the people who may add one (an admin signed in with GitHub, never the demo). It opens the same Add a product dialog as Settings > Products, then brings the person back to their exploration as they left it. The Library page has no rename any more: the library keeps its name (Settings > Products no longer offers Rename for it either). A product page no longer shows a pencil by its title: one "More actions" button at the end of the title's line holds Rename (the same rename in place) and Remove from Bridge. Removing asks for the product's name, typed exactly, says in plain words that Bridge deletes nothing on GitHub, and sends a review of the Bridge settings that drops only that product, the same path as a rename. Viewers who may not edit see no menu.
