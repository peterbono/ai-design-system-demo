A product added from its address keeps its developers' task in the design team's own code, where Being built follows it, but those developers usually work elsewhere. Once it is done exploring, team members get a quiet "Copy for your developers": on the exploration page right under the "Developers build it" step, which now says "Their developers work elsewhere: send them the message.", and on the same row in Being built. It copies one plain, self-contained message (what changes, the page today, the preview, the screenshot when Bridge kept one) with no tech words, then says "Message copied. Paste it where your developers work." Signed-out and sample visitors never see it, nor does a change that shipped, was declined or archived.
