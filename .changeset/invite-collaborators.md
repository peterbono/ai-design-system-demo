Invite collaborators: a dialog from the team menu and Settings that takes several work emails, a role picker (Can view, Can edit, Reviewer as Designer or Developer, Admin) and lists who has access with Resend. Settings > Members lists the team with a role and a More menu per person for admins (Make admin, Remove from team with a confirm step), and as read only text for everyone else. The sample team shows its sample people and opens the demo dialog on every change; a site without invites set up says so and sends nothing. New page /studio/invite/ where the invitation email leads.
