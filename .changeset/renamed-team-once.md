A team whose repository was renamed or moved to another owner now shows once, by its current name and with the higher role, in "Your teams" (the team menu), the "Use Bridge in Claude" and "Connect Figma" pickers, and the account's teams. Memberships, recent teams, names and roles this browser kept under the old name read as the current one, and a connection in Claude is always made for the current name; connections made before the rename keep working under it.
