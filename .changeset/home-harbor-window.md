Studio Home, nothing waiting: the Harbor zone becomes a window onto the harbor instead of a small drawing in its corner. The water runs across the whole zone along its bottom (one unbroken horizon, two rows of ripples, clipped by the rounded corners); the bridge stands on it in the right third and runs out of frame; the sailboat with the fisherman rides in the foreground left of it; gulls, the sun and a cloud fill the sky. The greeting sits above the water with its baseline on the top of the arch. A narrow zone lets the gulls go, a phone keeps only the water. `QuietScene` gains `layout="window"` (the inline drawing stays the default).
