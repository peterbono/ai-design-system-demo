The designers' copy of a product is a one-time team setup, decided once in Add a product ("Give designers their own copy"). Nobody sees "Make my copy" any more. Right after Add or Create product with the copy ticked, the product's page makes the copy in the background with the sign-in of the admin or lead who added it: one quiet line, "Getting a copy ready for your designers…", then nothing. If it could not be made, that person reads a calm line with Try again; designers never see it. When only a tick on GitHub lets Bridge open the new copy, an admin gets the one more step under the way to start, before the previews step. For a product added before this, "Start an exploration" makes the copy first, in the same action, with one short wait line, then starts. "Get the latest" is unchanged. Settings, onboarding and the MCP server now say Bridge makes the copy, never to look for "Make my copy".
