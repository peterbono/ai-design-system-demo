A design lead can sign in by email. An admin makes an email member a design lead in Settings, Members. They answer on explorations like any lead: Comment, Needs changes, Looks right, Not doing this, Take it back, and hand-over. The last review on the developers' code stays on GitHub. Through Bridge, only a lead can now write a lead's decision, their yes, Done exploring or the developers' task, on a team with design owners.
