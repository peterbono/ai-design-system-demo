Studio home on the demo shows the demo's real data to visitors: what waits for a review, recent changes, the library at a glance (components, tokens, contrast, adoption), read-only. The greeting says "You're exploring the Bridge demo" with one primary, Connect your design system, and the team launcher on demand. Every action that would change the demo (Create branch, New brand, Send suggestion, Approve, Start an exploration...) opens "Make it yours": sign in with GitHub, then connect your design system, or keep exploring. The demo is one list (lib/demo.ts DEMO_REPOS): the site's own repository and the hosted demo team. Owners with write access keep the normal Studio.
