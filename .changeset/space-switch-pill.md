Library and Studio headers: one switch replaces the "Open Studio" and "Library" buttons. A white pill names the space you are in, on a Deep Harbor track, with the other space as a drawn icon (pen and notes, open book). It carries the page's item across (Button in the Library opens Button in Studio, and back), keeps the team on team shells and team Libraries, and stays in the header on phones.
