Studio Overview sorts the to-do list by kind of work. "For you" lists only the decisions a designer or design system owner makes in the Studio, ordered by what unblocks the most: review the design of a change (it says when before and after are ready), ship an approved change, decide on a request, review usage guidance. Each button names the task ("Review design", "Ship", "Decide", "Review guidance") and the line under the title says what happens once it is done. Failing tests, a clash with the latest library, a missing release note, drafts, requested changes and the tech review fold into a quiet "Waiting on a developer" line that names the blocker and who is expected, with a link to the change only. The headline counts only what is for you ("2 decisions wait for you.", "Nothing needs you right now.").
