Measure triage on the full library. The measure: forced states apply to the component and everything in it (a field's Hover is its control's hover), logical properties (`padding-block-start`) are read in the cascade, a declaration whose variable does not give the computed value is not taken as the token, visually hidden text is not a layer, and a component that draws nothing itself is not split into instances. Pairing reads every class of a part, prefers the part's own parent, keeps classless decorations and same-word texts in another role unpaired. The status compares a variant with the closest story that renders its props, names an arg a story refuses, checks sets drawn under a folder (Progress/Linear) and names the open popup Figma draws. `--variants` measures every story as written; parts with no story of their own (MenuItem, MenuSubmenu, AccordionItem) are measured inside their component as `<title>/<Set>` entries, and every measured component, popups and parts included, carries the token values its Figma sets name (`resolved`).
