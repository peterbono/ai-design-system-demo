GitHub busy for the viewer's account is now a calm wait, never "Product not found", "Make my copy", an empty list or a role stuck on "Checking". One classification (lib/github-busy) reads 403 or 429 answers with no reads left, or in GitHub's limit words, as busy with the reset time. Team pages keep the team as last read in this browser, product pages keep the explorations as last read with one line ("GitHub is busy for your account. Bridge is back at 13:52."), Being built and team load errors say the same, and each reads again once on its own just after the reset (or on Try again). The team chip shows the last known role, else "Signed in". Core no longer reads a worded limit 403 as a missing design copy or a missing installation.
