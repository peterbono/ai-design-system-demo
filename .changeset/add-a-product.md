Add a product from Bridge: in Settings > Products and the onboarding Products step, an admin picks the product's code among what Bridge can see on GitHub, names it, and keeps the designers' copy. Bridge reads its previews from Vercel when they exist and sends the new product for review. The Components drawer counts a request as soon as it is sent, and the read-only banner no longer says "repository".
