Check the build: once the developers show their version of an exploration, its designer and the product manager of its product (the design lead when library pieces changed) compare it with what was approved and say Looks right or Needs changes (the build is off, or the design must change). Bridge keeps who approved which version and the Design check on it. The PM hears when a build is ready and when it shipped, and their Home lists what waits for their opinion.
