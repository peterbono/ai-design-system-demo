The Studio side nav has two groups. Products lists the team's products, the ones you explore in or opened lately first, five at most, then All products with the total when the team has more; the product you are on always shows. Design system holds Components, Tokens, Quality and Explorations (the library's own page, now titled "Library explorations"). Settings sits at the foot, and the phone menu shows the same groups. A team's /products/ address now opens All products: every product, yours first with a quiet "You explore here", the same status line as on Home, a "Find a product" filter and Show more for long lists, and Add a product for admins and leads. Local dev adds scenario S24, a team with fifteen products.
