Studio reviews suggestions. A suggestion that waits on you shows on Home and in the review queue next to branches, and counts in the Branches badge (one list for all three). It opens on its own card, in the queue or from Branches (`?suggestion=N`, and from a product branch's missing pieces): who suggests it, Before and After, three proof chips (design system checks, contrast in light and dark, where it is used, re-run on today's main), a big Approve with Suggest changes next to it, Decline in More actions, and the next items stacked behind. A suggestion approved for an older version, or one main moved under, says so first. Approve and Suggest changes show only to owners; everyone else reads why. Once approved, the card shows how a developer applies it. How review works has a Suggestions section.
