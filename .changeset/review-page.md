Studio branch review, redesigned: a breadcrumb (Branches, its product group, the title), the title with its status, and one line saying who asks and why. The change itself comes first, by type: guidance reads as it will in the Library with new text highlighted and click-to-edit (Save goes to the branch), tokens show before and after with the themes they change in, components show before and after screens with "Try it in the product". Below: the conversation (people only, latest comment inline, earlier ones on demand) and one line about checks with its details. One sticky decision bar: comment, "Suggest changes", "Approve" or "Approve and merge", and "More actions" (owner override only when it is the only way, share with a product team, copy a link to the review or for a developer, back to draft, close branch). Approving accepts a Claude guidance draft. A branch with several changes gets an outline with a "looked at" reading aid. The review queue uses the same view with its own bar (comment, Suggest changes, Approve, More actions with "Ask someone else to review"), outcome marks in its list, a toast with Undo after Approve, and A, S, J, K keys. "Open on GitHub" links leave this view.
