Every designer writes in the products through Bridge: a designer signed in with GitHub (push on the team, not an admin or a lead) now starts, saves, hands off and archives explorations in the design team's copy through Bridge's app, like an email designer, under the same policy (only their own explorations, never the default branch or a merge). They need no access to the copy on GitHub; leads and admins keep GitHub. The connector routes their product calls the same way, and Settings tells admins once.
