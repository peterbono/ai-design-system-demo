Review and New brand are real on team pages. Review lists what waits on you in the team's repository (its branches, suggestions, guidance drafts and contrast failures) and opens each one in place, with the same queue as the self-hosted Studio. New brand runs the four-step flow on the team's own tokens, brands and contrast pairs, and sends the brand the way the team's `changes` mode says: a suggestion, or a draft branch. In direct mode the first branch asks for Bridge Direct's OK before anything is sent. Drafts are kept per team in the browser.
