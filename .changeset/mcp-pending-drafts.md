Two tools to draft change texts from Figma with your own Claude: `bridge_pending_drafts` returns the drafts a designer is waiting for (changes, starting draft, notes, voice rules, output contract, and the before and after PNGs as image content) and `bridge_write_drafts` writes the titles and summaries back for the plugin to fill in. Say "Draft my Bridge changes".
