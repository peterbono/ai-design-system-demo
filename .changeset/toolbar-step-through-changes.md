The toolbar shows what changed without being asked. On a token change, opening the bar outlines every changed surface of the screen and counts them in the bar ("3 changes"); the count also turns the outlines off and on (C). Previous and Next scroll to each change in reading order (smooth, or instant with reduced motion), outline the current one stronger and announce it to screen readers. Isolate dims everything but the changes, off by default, and Esc turns it off. Outlines, position and Isolate hold on the Live product side, which shows the same elements. When nothing changes on the screen the bar says so and names the screens the change shows on, with a way to go there. The B shortcut to flip sides in place is shown in the side buttons' tooltip. The token swatches moved from the bar into the Changes sheet, with their before and after values, to keep the bar compact; at 390px the changes take a second row.
